**PANEL 1:**  
*Morning on the Milwaukee Riverwalk. The Bronze Fonz is feeling himself under a bright start, chin up like he personally arranged the sunshine.*

FONZ: "Ayyyy. Look at this. **High near 44.** Sun's out. Crowd's back. I told you the jacket was enough."

*A pigeon lands on his shoulder.*

FONZ: "Don't get comfortable, feathers. This is still Milwaukee in March. She's a heartbreaker."

---

**PANEL 2:**  
*A bundled-up guy with a coffee stops in front of the statue.*

GUY: "Fonzie, weren't we at, like, 70 earlier this week?"

FONZ: "Yeah. For five beautiful minutes we were all young again."

GUY: "So what happened?"

FONZ: "What happened? The calendar happened, pal. Also the atmosphere."

GUY: "Should I trust today?"

FONZ: "Correctamundo... until tonight."

---

**PANEL 3:**  
*The Fonz stares heroically down Third Street, which is unfortunate, because the interesting weather is behind him.*

FONZ: "Here's the setup. Daytime? Manageable. **West wind 5 to 10 mph.** Real civilized."

*A beat.*

FONZ: "Then after dark, this town starts freelancin'. **Rain moves in tonight, maybe a little snow mixed in, low around 34.**"

ANOTHER PIGEON: "..."

FONZ: "Don't look at me like that. I didn't book it."

---

**PANEL 4:**  
*Later. Clouds creep in. A tourist couple tries taking a selfie with the statue and accidentally gets a trash can blowing through the background.*

TOURIST: "Whoa! Is it supposed to get windy like that?"

FONZ: "Supposed to? Baby, this city's about to put on a clinic."

TOURIST'S WIFE: "How windy?"

FONZ: "**South winds 20 to 25, gusts up to 45 tonight.**"

TOURIST: "Forty-five?!"

FONZ: "That's not weather. That's a personal insult."

---

**PANEL 5:**  
*Night on the Riverwalk. Clouds own the sky now. A first sloppy drop hits the Bronze Fonz right on the nose.*

FONZ: "I knew this would happen. I finally start thinking maybe the jacket's enough, maybe the city and I are turning a corner, maybe this whole spring thing's got legs..."

*A wet snowflake follows the raindrop.*

FONZ: "And then Milwaukee says, 'Sit down, leather boy.'"

---

**PANEL 6:**  
*The Fonz holds the pose anyway, because of course he does.*

FONZ: "Lemme tell you something. A **44-degree** fake-out, a little rain, a little wet snow, some overnight chaos? That's not gonna break me."

*A beat.*

FONZ: "It is gonna freeze my bronze buns off."

*A beat.*

FONZ: "But metaphorically. Statue."

**END.**

*Stay cool, Milwaukee. The Fonz literally has no other choice.*
