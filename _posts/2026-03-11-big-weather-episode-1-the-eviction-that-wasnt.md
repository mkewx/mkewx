Monday walked into the Big Weather house like he already had the season wrapped up.

Sun was stretched across Milwaukee with **65 degrees**, smiling into the camera like the audience had already voted him America's Favorite Forecast.

---

**Diary Room — Sun**

"Look, y'all saw it. I came in here, gave the city **mid-60s**, people opening windows, people smiling at strangers, somebody grilling on a balcony in March. That's power."

He leaned back.

"I'm not saying I already won this game… but I'm not *not* saying it."

---

In the kitchen, Rain rolled his eyes.

"Oh he's feeling himself," Rain said quietly, stirring a mug nobody remembered him making. "One warm day and suddenly he's the mayor of Milwaukee."

Across the table sat Cold Snap.

Silent. Hoodie zipped up. Watching.

---

Tuesday afternoon, the house started changing.

Clouds moved in first, slow and smug, taking up the whole ceiling over the city.

Rain leaned against the counter, talking to the camera.

---

**Diary Room — Rain**

"You can't play this game like Sun does. Too flashy. Too confident."

He shrugged.

"You stay quiet. You wait. Then suddenly the house flips."

---

Sun still looked confident, but the smile had tightened a little.

"I mean yeah, the house feels different," he admitted. "But let's be honest. America loves sunshine."

From the hallway, Cold Snap laughed.

First time all season.

---

Wednesday morning arrived with the eviction results.

And Milwaukee couldn't believe it.

The house woke up to **rain and snow chances**, a **high near 40**, and the kind of cold, damp air that makes the whole city stare out the window like somebody just betrayed them personally.

Overnight, the temperature had slipped to around **33** as **rain changed to rain and snow**.

Rain stood in the living room with his arms crossed.

"I told y'all," he said.

"You don't throw a victory party in March."

---

Cold Snap stepped into the Diary Room.

"They tried to evict me," he said calmly.

He looked straight into the camera.

"But in this house?"

He smiled.

"I always survive."

---

Outside, Wind kicked the patio furniture just to stir things up.

Nobody in Milwaukee knew what Thursday's episode would bring.

But one thing was clear.

The villain survived eviction.

And the house had flipped.
