*INT. BIG WEATHER HOUSE – MORNING*

*The cameras come on. Contestants are scattered around the house. Outside the window, Milwaukee looks gray and undecided.*

**SUN:** Alright houseguests, let’s just say what everyone’s thinking. I’m back. Yesterday I had this city at **near 65 degrees**. People were outside. Sunglasses. Patio weather. Frankly? I carried the whole show.

**RAIN:** *(quietly stirring coffee)* You say that every time.

**SUN:** Because it’s true! I mean look at the numbers. Beautiful start to the week. Clear skies. Everybody thinks winter’s gone.

**WIND:** *(blowing through the living room)* Yeah, yeah, yeah. We’ve heard this speech before.

---

*Diary Room camera turns on.*

**SUN:** Look, every season someone tries to take me out early. But the viewers love me. You give Milwaukee a day in the **mid‑60s in March**, and suddenly everybody thinks I run the house.

---

*BACK IN THE HOUSE*

**RAIN:** Funny thing about this house… things change.

**SUN:** What’s that supposed to mean?

**RAIN:** Forecast says I start moving in tonight. By tomorrow there’s about a **50 percent chance of rain** around here.

**WIND:** *(grinning)* Oh this is getting good.

---

*Diary Room.*

**RAIN:** Sun talks a big game, but everyone knows the truth. One cloud deck and he disappears.

---

*BACK IN THE HOUSE*

**COLD SNAP** *(walking in slowly)* Did someone say disappear?

**SUN:** Oh come on. You again? I thought we voted you out weeks ago.

**COLD SNAP:** That's the funny thing about this house. Nobody actually leaves.

**WIND:** He’s not wrong.

---

*Diary Room.*

**COLD SNAP:** The house thinks I’m the villain. Fine by me. I’ve got patience. I wait for the right moment… then suddenly Milwaukee wakes up and it's **near 40 degrees** again.

---

*BACK IN THE HOUSE – EVENING*

*Clouds thicken outside the windows.*

**RAIN:** Looks like the house vote is shifting.

**SUN:** This is ridiculous. I had the numbers this morning!

**WIND:** Welcome to Big Weather, buddy.

---

Tonight inside the Big Weather house:

- Temperatures sliding toward the **upper 30s by morning**.

And just when the house thinks winter has finally been evicted…

*COLD SNAP smiles at the camera.*
