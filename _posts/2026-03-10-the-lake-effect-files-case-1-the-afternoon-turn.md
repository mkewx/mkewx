The morning started honest. I'll give it that.

Sun came up clean over the East Side, no fog, no games. Already **52 degrees by 8 a.m.** The kind of start that makes you trust a day. I poured my coffee, opened the blinds, and thought about taking a walk. Maybe down to the Third Ward. Maybe just around the block. Didn't matter. The sky was blue and the air felt like a promise.

I should've known better. Promises don't last in this town. Not in March.

By around 10 a.m. the clouds started creeping in from the southwest. Slow. Casual. Like they weren't up to anything. But I've been doing this long enough to read a sky, and that sky had guilt written all over it. **Winds shifted south, 10 to 15 mph.** The barometer started slipping. You don't notice it right away, but your bones do.

I checked the forecast. **High near 65**, they said. Maybe. If the sun holds. But the afternoon had other plans. **Rain moving in after noon. About a 50 percent chance, climbing to 60 by evening.** The models couldn't agree on the details, and when the models can't agree, that's when things get interesting.

Around 2 p.m., the first drops hit the windshield. Not hard. Not yet. Just enough to let you know the day had changed its mind.

I pulled my collar up. Not because I was cold. Old habit. Back home in Birmingham we had days like this too, where the morning lied to you with sunshine and the afternoon showed up with a whole different attitude. At least down there you could blame the Gulf. Up here, it's the lake. Always the lake. She runs this city's weather like a quiet boss who never raises her voice but always gets her way.

**Temperature dropped to the low 50s by 4 p.m.** The rain settled in. Not a downpour, just steady. The kind that soaks through your jacket before you realize you should've brought an umbrella. The streets got that shine to them, headlights stretching long across the wet pavement.

I ducked into a bar. Ordered something warm. Watched the rain run down the window and thought about how yesterday was perfect. **A clean 67 degrees and not a cloud in the sky.** That was Monday. This was Tuesday. And that's the thing about Milwaukee weather. It doesn't owe you consistency. It gives you a perfect day and then takes it back, just to remind you it can.

**Overnight lows dropping to the mid-30s.** The rain might mix with something colder before morning. Might not. The forecast is playing it close to the vest, and I've learned not to push when the weather doesn't want to talk.

Case stays open.
