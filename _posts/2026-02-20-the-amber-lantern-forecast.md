Once upon a shoreline, a little city woke to an amber sky.

<!--more-->

The wind came first, eager and sharp, tugging at scarves and rattling signs. Clouds wandered in behind it — not angry, just restless — and the air stayed crisp enough to make every breath feel like it matters.

Later, the sun peeks through in brief, dramatic cameos. No grand warmth, but a small reminder: winter can be polite sometimes.

**Forecast vibe:** brisk, partly cloudy, wind with attitude.  
**Move:** layers. Always layers.
