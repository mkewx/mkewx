*INT. BROCACH IRISH PUB - MORNING*

*MEG, DANA, TESS, MARCO, CHARLIE, and GLENN sit around their usual table near the window. Outside, clouds streak across the sky and trees bend sideways. A sandwich board on the sidewalk blows over.*

**CHARLIE:** Could this wind BE any more aggressive?

**DANA:** It's not that bad. It's actually **59°F** right now. That's warm for March.

**MARCO:** *(hair completely destroyed)* Tell that to my hair. I walked two blocks and now I look like I fought a car wash.

*Laugh track.*

---

*INT. BROCACH - CONTINUOUS*

**GLENN:** What's happening is a post-frontal pressure gradient. Yesterday's storm system moved through with all that rain and thunder, and now we're getting the backside. **Westerly winds, 15 to 25 mph.** Classic March pattern. The atmosphere is essentially exhaling.

**MEG:** *(not looking up from her phone)* Glenn. The atmosphere is not "exhaling."

**GLENN:** It's a simplified analogy for—

**MEG:** Nobody. Asked.

*Laugh track.*

**TESS:** I kind of love it, though. The wind feels alive. Like the city is shaking off winter. My knee agrees. Pressure's climbing back up.

**CHARLIE:** Your knee is not a weather instrument.

**TESS:** My knee predicted yesterday's thunderstorms twelve hours before your phone did.

**CHARLIE:** ...That's actually true.

---

*EXT. THE MILWAUKEE RIVERWALK - AFTERNOON*

*DANA and MEG walk along the river. The wind whips their scarves around. The sky is gray but bright, not threatening. It's the kind of cloudy where you can still feel the warmth.*

**MEG:** It's weird. It's cloudy but it doesn't feel cold at all.

**DANA:** Temps are already dropping, though. Gonna **fall to about 40** by tonight. **Low of 34.** This is the warmest it's gonna feel all day and we already missed the peak.

**MEG:** So what you're saying is we should stay outside right now.

**DANA:** What I'm saying is enjoy it before it's gone. Tomorrow's supposed to be gorgeous. **Sunny. Near 60 again.**

**MEG:** Milwaukee in March is like a relationship where they keep almost being perfect but not quite.

**DANA:** That's... weirdly accurate.

---

*INT. MARCO AND CHARLIE'S APARTMENT - EVENING*

*A small apartment above a bar on KK Avenue. The wind rattles the windows.*

**MARCO:** *(eating leftover cheese curds)* Is the building supposed to shake like this?

**CHARLIE:** **Gusts up to 25 mph.** Old buildings do that. It's character.

**MARCO:** Character is exposed brick. This is structural concern.

*Laugh track.*

---

*INT. BROCACH - NIGHT*

*The whole group is back. The wind has died down slightly. Through the window, partly cloudy skies show patches of stars.*

**TESS:** *(picks up guitar)* I wrote a new one. It's called "Blow Me Away."

**EVERYONE:** No.

**TESS:** *Blow me away, blow-ow me away, Milwaukee wind took my recycling bin todaaay...*

**GLENN:** Recycling pickup is actually Wednesday, so—

**MEG:** GLENN.

*Laugh track. Credits roll over a shot of the six of them walking out into the breezy night, jackets zipped, Charlie's hat blowing off his head and Marco chasing it down the street.*

**END.**

*We'll be there for you, Milwaukee. Even when the wind won't quit.*
