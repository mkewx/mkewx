*FADE IN.*

*INT. MILWAUKEE PUBLIC LIBRARY - CENTRAL BRANCH - SUNDAY MORNING*

*Five teenagers sit at separate tables in a reading room. Outside the tall windows, a blue sky stretches over downtown. It's gorgeous and they're stuck inside.*

*A librarian drops a stack of papers on the front desk.*

**LIBRARIAN:** You have until 4 PM. Write an essay about what you think today's weather means to you. I'll be in the back. Don't break anything.

*She leaves. Silence. Nobody moves.*

---

**THE JOCK** leans back in his chair, arms crossed. He's wearing a Marquette hoodie and shorts despite it being March.

**JOCK:** It's **60°F** out there. You know that? Sixty. I should be at Bradford Beach right now throwing a football. Instead I'm in here because Coach caught me skipping study hall.

**THE NERD:** *(without looking up)* It's actually forecast to reach **65°F** by this afternoon. **Southwest winds around 15 mph.** Which means it'll feel cooler than the actual temperature due to wind chill, but still well above the March average of 38. So technically, you're missing the best weather day Milwaukee has had since October.

**JOCK:** Thanks. That makes me feel so much better.

---

**THE PROM QUEEN** sits by the window, watching people walk dogs along Wisconsin Avenue.

**PROM QUEEN:** My mom made me come here because I got a B+ in AP Chemistry. A B+. She said I was "losing focus." I'm losing focus because it's Sunday and the sun is out and I can see people out there living their actual lives.

**THE BASKET CASE** pulls her hood down for the first time.

**BASKET CASE:** At least it's not raining. Yesterday it rained all day. Today it's like the city apologized.

*Everyone looks at her. She shrugs.*

**BASKET CASE:** What? I pay attention to things.

---

**THE REBEL** is carving something into the desk with a pen cap. He stops and looks out the window.

**REBEL:** My old man says March weather in Milwaukee is God flipping a coin every morning. Yesterday it was **55 and pouring.** Today it's blue skies and **60 degrees.** Tomorrow? Who knows. Maybe snow. Maybe seventy.

**NERD:** Statistically, there's a **20% chance of thunderstorms** tonight, but the overall trend through the weekend is warm and partly cloudy. **Pressure is steady at 29.3 inches.** The pattern actually suggests—

**REBEL:** Dude. I was having a moment.

**NERD:** Sorry.

---

*LATER. They've migrated to a single table. The sun slants through the windows, warm and golden. The Jock has his chair tilted back. The Prom Queen has taken off her jacket. The Basket Case is sketching the skyline outside.*

**JOCK:** You know what's weird? This weather makes me not hate this.

**PROM QUEEN:** Being in detention?

**JOCK:** Being *here.* With you guys. On a day like this. It's like... if it was cold and gray out there, I'd be miserable. But the sun coming through those windows? It changes it. I don't know.

**BASKET CASE:** That's not the weather. That's just you being less of a jerk than usual.

*Laugh. Even the Rebel cracks a smile.*

**NERD:** For what it's worth, the **high of 60°F** with clear skies and light winds? This is objectively one of the nicest days we'll have this month. So if you had to pick a Sunday to be stuck inside with four strangers, you picked a good one. Because you can at least see what you're missing.

**REBEL:** *(beat)* That's somehow the most depressing and hopeful thing anyone's ever said to me.

---

*EXT. MILWAUKEE PUBLIC LIBRARY - 4:00 PM*

*They walk out together. The sun is low and golden over the city. **57°F** and dropping. The wind has picked up a little. They stand on the steps for a moment, not quite ready to leave.*

**PROM QUEEN:** So. Same time next week?

**JOCK:** God, I hope not.

**BASKET CASE:** *(smiling)* I wouldn't mind it.

*They scatter into the city. The camera pulls back. Milwaukee stretches out below, warm and bright and completely unaware that five kids just figured something out about themselves in a reading room.*

*FADE OUT.*

*Dear Milwaukee: We accept the fact that we had to sacrifice a whole Sunday checking the forecast. But we think you're crazy for making us write an essay telling you who we think we are. You see us as you want to see us. In the simplest terms. The most convenient definitions. But what we found out is that each one of us is a forecaster. And a comedian. And a skeptic. And a romantic. And a rebel.*

*Does that answer your question?*

*Sincerely yours, the Sunday Detention Club.*
