---

**PANEL 1:**  
*Morning on the Milwaukee Riverwalk. The Bronze Fonz is glowing in the sunshine like he personally negotiated it.*

FONZ: "Ayyyy. Now this is a setup I understand. **High near 44 degrees.** Sun's out. Leather's holding. Crowd's admiring. Very nice. Very, very nice."

*A pigeon lands on his shoulder.*

FONZ: "Careful, pal. I already worked with one bird. His name was Potsie."

---

**PANEL 2:**  
*A guy walking to work slows down with a coffee in his hand.*

GUY: "Fonzie, didn't we hit 70 earlier this week?"

FONZ: "Yeah. Gorgeous, wasn't it? For a minute there I thought Milwaukee finally decided to stop embarrassing itself."

GUY: "So this is still okay, right?"

FONZ: "Correctamundo. Daytime's respectable. **West wind around 5 to 10 mph.** Real easy stuff. This is jukebox weather."

GUY: "Jukebox weather?"

FONZ: "You wouldn't get it, pal."

---

**PANEL 3:**  
*The Fonz squints heroically into the distance. This is complicated by the fact that he is still bolted facing the wrong way.*

FONZ: "You know what really stings? Back at Arnold's, if the weather turned, I could step inside. Hit the men’s room, fix the hair, regroup, maybe let Richie panic for everybody."

*A beat.*

FONZ: "Now? No mirror. No bathroom. No turning around. Third Street in front of me, river behind me, pigeons all over payroll. That's progress?"

---

**PANEL 4:**  
*Clouds start creeping in over downtown. A tourist couple walks up mid-selfie.*

TOURIST: "Excuse me, are you supposed to be a Happy Days statue?"

FONZ: "Baby, I'm *the* Happy Days statue."

WIFE: "What's the forecast tonight?"

FONZ: "Tonight? That's where the writers get mean. **Rain moves in late, maybe a little snow mixed in after midnight, low around 34.**"

TOURIST: "Snow? After this week?"

FONZ: "Welcome to Milwaukee. One day it's spring, next day it's a personal prank."

---

**PANEL 5:**  
*A second pigeon lands. The Fonz looks deeply offended.*

FONZ: "I can feel the pressure dropping too. **About 30.1 inches and falling.**"

PIGEON: "..."

FONZ: "Don't '...' me. I know a setup when I see one. This whole day is walking around in Joanie-and-Chachi levels of false confidence."

---

**PANEL 6:**  
*Evening. The wind starts kicking up trash and rattling signs along the Riverwalk.*

LOCAL WOMAN: "Fonzie, how bad's it getting?"

FONZ: "Let's just say this ain't a sock hop. **South winds 20 to 25 mph, gusts up to 45 tonight.** Rain, maybe wet snow, a whole lotta attitude."

LOCAL WOMAN: "So the jacket isn't enough?"

FONZ: "Lady, the jacket is enough for *me.* The question is whether the city deserves it."

---

**PANEL 7:**  
*Night. The first cold drop hits his bronze nose. Then a wet flake. The Fonz stares straight ahead like a man betrayed by both heaven and production.*

FONZ: "I had a dream, you know. Thought maybe after that 70-degree tease, this town and I were headed somewhere."

*A beat.*

FONZ: "Then Milwaukee looked me dead in the thumbs and said, 'Sit down, leather boy.'"

*A longer beat.*

FONZ: "Still cool, though."

**END.**

*Stay loose, Milwaukee. And maybe don't trust a warm streak in March unless Mrs. Cunningham personally signs off on it.*
