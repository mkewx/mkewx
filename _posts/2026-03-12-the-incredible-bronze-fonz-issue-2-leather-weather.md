**PANEL 1:**  
*Morning on the Milwaukee Riverwalk. The Bronze Fonz stands under a clean, bright sky, trying very hard to look unbothered.*

FONZ: "Ayyyy. Sun's out. **High near 44 degrees.** Little chilly, sure, but that's workable. Leather's handled worse."

*A beat.*

FONZ: "Well. Leather's *seen* worse. I'm a statue. I don't actually get options."

---

**PANEL 2:**  
*A bundled-up local walks past with coffee and gives the statue a skeptical look.*

LOCAL: "Fonzie, is that jacket really enough for today?"

FONZ: "Correctamundo. Daytime's alright. **West wind around 5 to 10 mph.** Nice little break in the action."

LOCAL: "So winter's over?"

FONZ: "Whoa, whoa, whoa. Nobody said that. This is Milwaukee in March, not Miami in May."

---

**PANEL 3:**  
*Early afternoon. The sun is fading behind thicker clouds. A pigeon lands on his shoulder like it pays rent there.*

FONZ: "See this? This is how they get ya. Nice morning, decent afternoon, everybody starts talking brave."

PIGEON: "..."

FONZ: "Then tonight the whole script changes. **Rain and snow moving in after midnight, low around 34,** and the wind starts acting like it owns the place."

*A beat.*

FONZ: "Pressure's sitting around **30.1 inches and falling.** I can feel it in my bronze kneecaps. Also in my bronze everything else."

---

**PANEL 4:**  
*Evening on the Riverwalk. The clouds have fully taken over. A tourist couple squints at the sky, then at the statue.*

TOURIST: "Wait, I thought it was supposed to be a nice day."

FONZ: "It was! For, like, five minutes. Then Milwaukee remembered what month it is."

TOURIST'S WIFE: "What's tonight looking like?"

FONZ: "Windy. **South winds building to 20 to 25 mph, gusts up to 45.** Little rain, little snow, little chaos. Real classy stuff."

---

**PANEL 5:**  
*Late night. The Riverwalk is empty. The Fonz stares straight ahead into a future he cannot physically avoid.*

FONZ: "I had one week. One beautiful little stretch where I thought maybe this jacket and I were headed for easier times."

*A snowflake lands on his nose.*

FONZ: "Guess not."

*A beat.*

FONZ: "Still... a leather jacket in Milwaukee in March? That's cool. That's eternal. That's the Fonz, pal."

**END.**

*Stay loose, Milwaukee. But maybe keep the winter coat handy anyway.*
