**PANEL 1:**
*Dense fog blankets the Milwaukee Riverwalk. The Bronze Fonz is barely visible. Just thumbs and a vague silhouette.*

FONZ: "You know, at Arnold's, the bathroom used to steam up from the kitchen. I had a system. Wipe the mirror left to right, check the hair, adjust, repeat. Professional stuff."

*A beat.*

FONZ: "Now look at me. Can't even see my own thumbs."

---

**PANEL 2:**
*A jogger materializes from the fog, startled to find herself inches from the statue.*

JOGGER: "OH! God. You scared me."

FONZ: "Whoa, easy. You almost ran into the Fonz. That's a **40°F** morning and a bruised kneecap you don't need."

JOGGER: "Why are you... wet?"

FONZ: "It's fog. The whole riverwalk is fogged in. **NE winds around 5 mph.** Visibility's garbage. Kinda romantic, though, if you think about it."

JOGGER: "I don't think about it."

FONZ: "Fair enough."

---

**PANEL 3:**
*A tourist couple approaches with a map, squinting at their phones.*

TOURIST: "Honey, I think the statue is over here somewhere..."

FONZ: "Right here, pal. You're basically standing on my feet."

TOURIST: "Is that...?"

FONZ: "The Fonz. In the bronze. Giving you your forecast. **Clouds all afternoon, low tonight around 35°F.** Pressure sitting at **30.02 inches** and holding. You're welcome."

WIFE: "Can we get a picture?"

FONZ: "In this fog? You'll get a picture of my left thumb and a lot of gray. But sure. I don't charge."

---

**PANEL 4:**
*Late afternoon. The fog has mostly burned off, revealing overcast skies. The Fonz stares at the river behind him. Wait. He can't. He's facing the wrong way.*

FONZ: "You know what bugs me? The fog clears and I still can't see the water. They bolted me facing Third Street. Third Street! You know what's on Third Street? Buildings. You know what's behind me? The entire Milwaukee River."

*A pigeon lands on his shoulder.*

FONZ: "This is my life now. Pigeons and a view of a parking ramp."

---

**PANEL 5:**
*Evening. The clouds thicken again. A bar regular stumbles out of a nearby pub.*

BAR GUY: "Heyyy, Fonzie!"

FONZ: "Ayyyy."

BAR GUY: "Cold tonight?"

FONZ: "**Dropping to the mid-30s.** Fog's rolling back in after midnight. Watch your step out there. The riverwalk gets slick."

BAR GUY: "You're alright, Fonz."

FONZ: "I know."

*Bar guy finger-guns at the statue and walks off into the mist.*

**END.**

*Stay cool, Milwaukee. Or at least stay visible.*
