**PANEL 1:**
*The Bronze Fonz stands on the Milwaukee Riverwalk. It's early morning. A jogger passes by shivering. The Fonz stares straight ahead at the buildings across the river.*

FONZ: "You know, my old office at Arnold's had a mirror, a sink, and four walls. Now I got a riverwalk and a bunch of seagulls. Real upgrade."

*A beat.*

FONZ: "I can't even see my hair. They pointed me at the buildings. The river's *behind* me. You believe that?"

JOGGER: "Are you... okay?"

FONZ: "I'm bronze, I'm bolted down, and it's **31°F** out here. What do you think?"

---

**PANEL 2:**
*A woman walks by bundled in a parka. The sun is shining.*

FONZ: "Looking mostly sunny today, gorgeous. **West winds 5 to 10 mph.** Perfect weather for a stroll along the river."

WOMAN: "Are you... giving me a weather forecast?"

FONZ: "I'm giving you *the* weather forecast. The Fonz doesn't do small talk. Sit on it."

---

**PANEL 3:**
*A dad and kid stop to take a photo with the statue. The dad checks his phone.*

DAD: "Says here the pressure's dropping. **29.4 inches** and falling."

FONZ: "Yeah, snow's moving in tonight. **40% chance** after dark. Could get slick out there."

KID: "Dad, is the statue talking?"

DAD: "Just smile for the picture, buddy."

---

**PANEL 4:**
*Night falls on the Riverwalk. Snow begins to drift down. The Fonz is alone, covered in a thin dusting of white.*

FONZ: "Low of **22°F** tonight. Clouds rolling in. Little snow on the ground."

*A beat.*

FONZ: "Not gonna lie, I could use a jacket. But the Fonz doesn't ask for help."

*Another beat.*

FONZ: "...If someone left a scarf out here, though, I wouldn't be mad about it."

---

**PANEL 5:**
*Morning. The snow stopped. A city worker brushes off the statue with a broom.*

FONZ: "Ayyyy. Thanks, pal. Tell Milwaukee to layer up today. And maybe check on their bronze friends once in a while."

*WORKER stares. Walks away faster.*

**END.**

*Stay cool, Milwaukee. Or don't. You don't really have a choice this week.*
