*FADE IN.*

*EXT. THE PFISTER HOTEL - FRIDAY MORNING*

The doors groan open.

A bus labeled CAMP MILWAUKEE hisses to a stop.

A counselor steps out first, too cheerful for the situation.

Behind him, a bunch of camp kids drag duffel bags into the lobby while the wind howls down Wisconsin Avenue like something large and annoyed just woke up.

**KID #1:** This was supposed to be camp.

**COUNSELOR:** It is camp.

**KID #2:** This is a haunted hotel.

**COUNSELOR:** It's a *historic* hotel.

A flag outside snaps so hard it sounds like a gunshot.

Everyone screams.

---

*INT. PFISTER LOBBY*

At the front desk stands a pale clerk in a neat jacket.

His nametag reads:

**JASON**

He doesn't blink nearly enough.

**JASON:** Welcome. Checkout is at 11. Survival is day to day.

**KID #3:** ...What?

**JASON:** Also, **high near 43 today. West winds 25 to 30 miles per hour. Gusts up to 45.**

A lamp flickers.

**COUNSELOR:** Why would you say that like a threat?

**JASON:** Because you booked a Friday the 13th in Milwaukee.

---

*INT. HALLWAY - LATER*

The kids creep through the Pfister like they're in a movie they absolutely should not be in.

One opens a curtain.

Outside, a patio chair skids down the street by itself.

**KID #1:** Did you see that?!

**KID #2:** Was that the wind?

**KID #3:** I don't know, but it just took somebody's brunch plans with it.

From the far end of the hallway comes a terrible scraping sound.

The counselor turns.

His rain poncho is gone.

Then his clipboard is gone.

Then, somehow, he is gone too.

**KID #1:** ...Did Jason get him?

**JASON:** *(appearing behind them)* No.

*A beat.*

**JASON:** The gusts did.

Everybody screams again.

---

*INT. HOTEL ROOM - AFTERNOON*

The kids have shoved hotel chairs against the windows like that helps against meteorology.

**KID #2:** So that's the killer? Wind?

**JASON:** Wind. Sudden noises. Milwaukee in March. False hope. It's an ensemble piece.

**KID #3:** Is there blood?

**JASON:** No, but there was a very graphic patio umbrella incident.

He parts the curtains.

The city looks partly sunny now, but mean about it.

**JASON:** That's how it gets you. Last night's rain is gone. The pressure's rising. The sky brightens. You think the danger passed."

**KID #1:** It didn't?

**JASON:** Have you heard outside?

A metal sign slams somewhere down the block.

Everybody jumps.

---

*INT. PFISTER ELEVATOR - SUNSET*

The lights flicker.

The elevator dings for no reason.

The missing counselor is suddenly inside, hair destroyed, eyes hollow.

**COUNSELOR:** I saw things out there.

**KID #2:** Oh my God. Was it Jason?

**COUNSELOR:** Worse.

*A beat.*

**COUNSELOR:** Horizontal garbage cans.

---

*INT. PFISTER LOBBY - NIGHT*

The camp kids sit wrapped in hotel blankets. Shaken. Humbled. Changed.

Jason slides a room key across the desk like he's passing judgment.

**JASON:** Tonight goes **mostly cloudy with a low around 27.** Quieter. Briefly.

**KID #3:** Briefly?

Jason turns toward the window.

Somewhere in the distance, Saturday night's snowstorm sharpens a machete.

**JASON:** In this franchise, the sequel always comes.

*SMASH CUT TO BLACK.*

**END.**

*Sleep tight, Milwaukee. And if you hear banging outside, it's probably just the wind. Probably.*
