*FADE IN.*

*EXT. THE PFISTER HOTEL - PREDAWN*

The street is empty. The marquee glows. Somewhere above Wisconsin Avenue, a loose sign rattles in the dark like it knows something.

A van marked CAMP MILWAUKEE pulls up to the curb.

A bunch of overconfident camp kids pile out, sleeping bags in hand.

**KID #1:** Why are we doing a camp sleepover in a hotel?

**COUNSELOR:** Budget cuts.

**KID #2:** This place looks haunted.

**COUNSELOR:** It's the Pfister. Everything looks haunted before sunrise.

A gust of wind barrels down the block and nearly takes a duffel bag with it.

---

*INT. PFISTER LOBBY - MORNING*

The revolving door shudders. Outside, the city is already starting to look mean.

At the front desk stands a pale young man in a nametag.

It reads: **JASON.**

**KID #3:** Why's he looking at us like that?

**KID #1:** Because it's Friday the 13th.

**JASON:** *(calmly)* Checkout is at 11. Also, **high near 43 today.** Very windy.

**COUNSELOR:** ...What?

**JASON:** **West winds 25 to 30 mph. Gusts up to 45.** Partly sunny by afternoon. Maybe a stray rain or wet snow shower if the atmosphere feels dramatic.

A lamp flickers.

---

*INT. HOTEL HALLWAY - LATER*

The camp kids creep down the corridor like they're in a movie they absolutely should not be in.

One kid peeks through a curtain.

**KID #2:** I don't see a monster.

**KID #3:** The monster is the wind, dude.

Right on cue, something outside slams hard enough to make everybody jump.

**KID #1:** What was that?!

**JASON:** *(appearing behind them somehow)* Patio furniture.

Everyone screams.

---

*INT. HOTEL ROOM - AFTERNOON*

The kids have built a blanket fort against the windows like it's going to help.

**KID #2:** So that's it? No killer? No curse? No blood on the walls?

**JASON:** No. Just Milwaukee in March.

He pulls back the curtain.

The flags outside are snapping sideways.

**JASON:** **Pressure around 30.2 inches and rising.** That's your clue the worst of last night's mess is out, but the backside's still got teeth.

**COUNSELOR:** That's actually... helpful.

**JASON:** I contain multitudes.

---

*INT. PFISTER LOBBY - NIGHT*

The wind finally eases a little. The worst of the day is gone.

The camp kids sit in the lobby wrapped in hotel blankets, shell-shocked but alive.

**KID #3:** So what's tonight?

**JASON:** **Mostly cloudy. Low around 27.** Less drama. For now.

**KID #1:** You say that like something else is coming.

Jason looks toward the window.

Far off in the dark, the weekend storm is sharpening its knives.

**JASON:** In this town? There's always a sequel.

*SMASH CUT TO BLACK.*

**END.**

*Sleep tight, Milwaukee. And don't trust a calm forecast in mid-March.*
