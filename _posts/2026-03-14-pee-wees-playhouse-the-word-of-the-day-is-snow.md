*INT. PLAYHOUSE - SATURDAY MORNING*

Pee-wee bursts into the room like he got shot out of a confetti cannon.

**PEE-WEE:** Good morning, Playhouse! Good morning, Chairry! Good morning, Conky! Good morning, Globey! Good morning, Cowboy Curtis!

**CHAIRRY:** Good morning, Pee-wee!

**CONKY:** Conky has important weather information! BEEP! BEEP! Today in Milwaukee: **cloudy, high near 38 degrees!**

**PEE-WEE:** That's not bad! That's not bad at all! That's what I call sweater weather!

**MISS YVONNE:** Sweater weather is acceptable as long as the sweater is beautiful.

**COWBOY CURTIS:** I can work with **upper 30s.** That's porch-sittin' temperature in a city that likes to argue.

---

*Magic Screen lights up with a map of Milwaukee.*

**MAGIC SCREEN:** But wait! There's more!

**PEE-WEE:** More? On a Saturday?

**MAGIC SCREEN:** A few snow showers or flurries are possible later today. Nothing too bossy at first. But tonight gets more serious.

**CONKY:** BEEP! BEEP! **Snow likely tonight, low around 31, east wind 5 to 15 mph!** Potential winter storm behavior detected!

**CHAIRRY:** Ooooh, I don't like it when the forecast says "potential." That's such a nervous word.

---

*A jeweled box glows. Jambi appears.*

**JAMBI:** Mecca lecca hi, mecca hiney ho!

**PEE-WEE:** Jambi! I wish Milwaukee forecasts in March would make up their mind!

**JAMBI:** Wish granted!

*Everybody waits.*

Nothing happens.

**PEE-WEE:** ...Jambi?

**JAMBI:** Even I have limits, Pee-wee.

---

*Miss Yvonne checks herself in a mirror.*

**MISS YVONNE:** Snow is terribly rude to hair.

**COWBOY CURTIS:** So what's the move?

**CONKY:** BEEP! BEEP! Daytime looks manageable. Nighttime looks trickier. Some forecasts are eyeing **3 to 5 inches of snow by late Saturday night into Sunday,** but the exact track can still wobble around because March likes attention.

**PEE-WEE:** So the weather is being silly.

**GLOBEY:** That is correct, Pee-wee.

---

**PEE-WEE:** I know what we need. The Word of the Day!

**EVERYONE:** The Word of the Day?!

**PEE-WEE:** Yes! And today's word is...

*A drumroll somehow happens even though nobody started it.*

**PEE-WEE:** **SNOW!**

Everybody in the Playhouse loses their minds.

**CHAIRRY:** SNOW!

**CONKY:** SNOW! BEEP! BEEP!

**MISS YVONNE:** Not on my shoes!

**COWBOY CURTIS:** That's Milwaukee, baby.

---

*Pee-wee looks out the window at the cloudy sky.*

**PEE-WEE:** So here's the deal, Milwaukee. Enjoy the daytime lull. Keep the cocoa close. And maybe don't get too cocky just because the morning looks harmless.

**CONKY:** Final report! **Pressure near 30.2 inches and steady, maybe falling late.** Translation: the Playhouse would like everybody to stay flexible.

**PEE-WEE:** Ha-ha! Forecasting is fun!

**CHAIRRY:** Easy for you to say. You don't have to shovel.

**END.**

*Today's secret word was snow. Sorry in advance, Milwaukee.*
