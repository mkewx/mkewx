*INT. PLAYHOUSE - SATURDAY MORNING*

Pee-wee Herman bursts into the Playhouse in boots that are trying way too hard.

**PEE-WEE:** Good morning, Chairry! Good morning, Conky! Good morning, Cowboy Curtis! Good morning, Miss Yvonne! Good morning, Magic Screen! Good morning, Pterri! Good morning, Globey!

**CHAIRRY:** Good morning, Pee-wee!

**PTERRI:** I had a bad feeling when I woke up! And then I had another bad feeling! And then I had toast!

**PEE-WEE:** Ha-ha! That's breakfast fear!

---

**CONKY:** BEEP BEEP! Conky has the weather! Conky has the weather! And the weather is acting suspicious!

**PEE-WEE:** Uh-oh! How suspicious?

**CONKY:** Today in Milwaukee: **cloudy, high near 40! Slight chance of snow this afternoon!**

**PEE-WEE:** That's not so bad.

**CHAIRRY:** That's what Milwaukee *wants* you to think.

**COWBOY CURTIS:** Sneaky weather. Classic.

---

*The Magic Screen lights up with downtown Milwaukee under a flat gray sky.*

**MAGIC SCREEN:** I have a picture! I have a picture!

**PEE-WEE:** Show me! Show me!

**MAGIC SCREEN:** The city has one of those looks.

**PEE-WEE:** What looks?

**MAGIC SCREEN:** The kind where it says, 'Nothing's wrong,' but it's already hiding snow behind its back.

**MISS YVONNE:** That is exactly the kind of weather that ruins a beautiful outfit.

---

*A jeweled box glows. Jambi appears.*

**JAMBI:** Mecca lecca hi, mecca hiney ho!

**PEE-WEE:** Jambi! I wish March in Milwaukee would stop being weird!

**JAMBI:** I cannot do that, Pee-wee.

**PEE-WEE:** You didn't even think about it!

**JAMBI:** I thought about it very quickly.

---

**CONKY:** BEEP BEEP! Nighttime is where the trouble starts! **Snow likely after 1 a.m.! Low around 31!** Maybe a little rain mixes in later! Forecast confidence: jumpy!

**PTERRI:** Jumpy?! I don't like jumpy! Jumpy is one step away from disaster!

**GLOBEY:** In Wisconsin, that is technically correct.

**COWBOY CURTIS:** So this is one of those 'looks fine now, weird later' situations.

**CONKY:** BEEP! Yes!

---

**MISS YVONNE:** How much snow are we talking about?

**CONKY:** Some forecasts are still wobbling around! A few inches are possible by late tonight into Sunday, but the exact track could still scoot around because March is a show-off!

**PEE-WEE:** I *knew* it! The weather is trying to be the center of attention!

**CHAIRRY:** It already is, Pee-wee!

---

**PEE-WEE:** I know what this day needs.

**EVERYONE:** What?!

**PEE-WEE:** The Secret Word!

**EVERYONE:** AAAAAHHHHHH!

**PEE-WEE:** Today's secret word is...

*A drumroll starts for no reason at all.*

**PEE-WEE:** **SNOW!**

Everybody screams.

**CHAIRRY:** SNOW!

**PTERRI:** SNOW! I KNEW IT! I KNEW IT FROM THE TOAST!

**MISS YVONNE:** Not on suede!

**COWBOY CURTIS:** Cover the grill!

**CONKY:** SNOW! BEEP! SNOW! BEEP!

---

*Pee-wee goes to the window and squints out at the sky like he's trying to out-weird it.*

**PEE-WEE:** Okay, here's the plan. Daytime: don't panic. Nighttime: panic a *little.* Keep the cocoa nearby, keep the boots where you can find them, and do not let one quiet gray afternoon talk you into bad decisions.

**CHAIRRY:** That's excellent advice.

**PEE-WEE:** I know! I give it to myself all the time!

**MAGIC SCREEN:** I have another picture!

**PEE-WEE:** What is it?

**MAGIC SCREEN:** Boots.

**PEE-WEE:** Perfect.

**END.**

*The secret word was snow, and everybody was right to scream.*
