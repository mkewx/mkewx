*FADE IN.*

*EXT. AMBASSADOR HOTEL - FRIDAY MORNING*

The neon hums.

The wind does not hum. It screams.

A rattling old school bus labeled CAMP NORTHWOODS YOUTH RETREAT lurches to the curb outside the Ambassador Hotel. The doors fold open, and a dozen camp kids step into downtown Milwaukee looking like they expected pine trees and got a city ghost story instead.

**KID #1:** Why are we here?

**COUNSELOR:** Long story.

**KID #2:** This is not camp.

**COUNSELOR:** It's character-building.

A plastic bag whips across Wisconsin Avenue like it knows something.

---

*INT. AMBASSADOR LOBBY - MOMENTS LATER*

The front desk clerk slides over a brass key.

**CLERK:** Room 507.

**COUNSELOR:** Great. We just need one room for the group.

The clerk looks up slowly.

**CLERK:** You booked the Dahmer room.

Silence.

**KID #3:** The what room?

**COUNSELOR:** The historical room.

**CLERK:** That is not what anyone calls it.

Thunder doesn't rumble. It doesn't need to. The front doors shake hard in a gust.

**CLERK:** Also, **high near 43 today. West winds 25 to 30 mph. Gusts up to 45.** Just in case you were hoping for peace.

---

*INT. ROOM 507 - LATE MORNING*

The room is too still.

There are two beds, one lamp, heavy curtains, and the sort of wallpaper that makes every bad decision look permanent.

A kid opens the curtains.

The windows moan.

**KID #1:** This room feels wrong.

**KID #2:** It's because of the serial killer thing.

**KID #3:** No, it's because the radiator sounds like somebody breathing through a hockey mask.

They all freeze.

The radiator hisses again.

**COUNSELOR:** That's steam.

A beat.

**COUNSELOR:** Probably.

---

*INT. ROOM 507 - AFTERNOON*

Outside, the sky gets brighter, which somehow makes everything worse.

The camp kids sit in a circle on the carpet like they're trying to survive a slumber party and a haunting at the same time.

**KID #2:** I thought scary weather was supposed to be storms and lightning.

**KID #1:** No. This is 1980s scary. Wind. Empty hallways. Bad carpeting. One room nobody should've booked.

The bathroom door creaks open by itself.

Everybody screams.

Nothing is there.

A beat later, the ice bucket slides two inches across the dresser.

**KID #3:** NOPE.

**COUNSELOR:** Stay calm!

A magazine cover tears loose from the table and slaps into the far wall.

**KID #2:** THE WIND IS IN THE ROOM.

---

*INT. HOTEL HALLWAY - SUNSET*

The hall is long, red, and mean-looking. A single bulb flickers.

The camp kids shuffle toward the elevator as if maybe the building won't notice them if they move quietly enough.

From somewhere down the corridor comes the soft drag of something heavy.

**KID #1:** Did you hear that?

**KID #3:** I heard that.

At the end of the hallway, hanging on a housekeeping cart, is a white maintenance face shield.

Not a hockey mask. Not exactly.

Close enough.

The elevator dings all by itself.

Everybody loses their minds.

---

*INT. ROOM 507 - NIGHT*

The worst of the daylight is gone. That's not comforting.

Outside, the wind still hammers the city, but the room has gone quieter now, which is somehow much more upsetting.

The counselor reads from the weather app with the voice of a man trying not to sound afraid.

**COUNSELOR:** Tonight goes **mostly cloudy with a low around 27.** Less wind later. That's good.

The lamp goes out.

A beat.

The lamp comes back on.

**KID #2:** That wasn't weather.

**KID #1:** No, but the weather's helping.

The window rattles once more.

Far away, somewhere past the hotel walls, Saturday night's storm is still out there, still forming, still deciding what kind of sequel it wants to be.

**KID #3:** Are we sleeping in shifts?

**COUNSELOR:** Absolutely.

*SMASH CUT TO BLACK.*

**END.**

*Friday the 13th came to Milwaukee with high winds, a haunted-feeling hotel room, and the strong possibility that nobody in Room 507 slept a wink.*
