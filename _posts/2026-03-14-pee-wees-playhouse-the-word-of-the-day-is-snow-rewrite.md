*INT. PLAYHOUSE - SATURDAY MORNING*

Pee-wee Herman bursts into the Playhouse carrying a mitten, a mug of cocoa, and way too much confidence.

**PEE-WEE:** Good morning, Chairry! Good morning, Conky! Good morning, Magic Screen! Good morning, Cowboy Curtis! Good morning, Miss Yvonne! Good morning, Globey! Good morning, everybody in Milwaukee trying to pretend this forecast is no big deal!

**CHAIRRY:** Eeeeeee! Good morning, Pee-wee!

**CONKY:** BEEP BEEP! Conky has the weather! Conky has the weather! Conky is emotionally prepared, which is more than can be said for Milwaukee!

**PEE-WEE:** Ha-ha! Hit me, Conky!

**CONKY:** Today: **cloudy, high near 40! Slight chance of snow after 1 p.m.!**

**PEE-WEE:** That's not terrible!

**CHAIRRY:** That's not good either, Pee-wee.

---

*The Magic Screen lights up.*

**MAGIC SCREEN:** I have a picture! I have a picture!

**PEE-WEE:** Let me see! Let me see!

On the screen: downtown Milwaukee under a gray sky, looking like the city knows it's being set up.

**MAGIC SCREEN:** The daytime forecast is being sneaky.

**COWBOY CURTIS:** Sneaky weather's the worst weather.

**MISS YVONNE:** No, the worst weather is any weather that frizzes.

**CONKY:** BEEP! Tonight is the interesting part! **Snow likely after 1 a.m.! Low around 31!** Maybe rain mixes in toward morning! Forecast confidence: dramatic, but not fully trustworthy!

**PEE-WEE:** So it's one of *those* March days.

**GLOBEY:** In Wisconsin, that narrows it down to all of them.

---

*A jeweled box glows. Jambi appears.*

**JAMBI:** Mecca lecca hi, mecca hiney ho!

**PEE-WEE:** Jambi! I wish weather forecasters could tell me exactly how much snow Milwaukee gets before I brag about it to everybody!

**JAMBI:** Wish... granted!

Everybody waits.

Nothing happens.

**PEE-WEE:** Jambi?

**JAMBI:** Some wishes are above my pay grade.

---

*Pterri flaps in, already upset.*

**PTERRI:** Is it a big storm? Is it a little storm? I don't like uncertainty!

**CONKY:** BEEP! BEEP! Some forecasts suggest a few inches are possible by late Saturday night into Sunday, but the exact track can still wobble! That means nobody should get cocky! Especially not in March!

**PEE-WEE:** I wasn't getting cocky.

*A beat.*

**PEE-WEE:** I was getting medium cocky.

---

**MISS YVONNE:** Pee-wee, should I wear the beautiful boots or the sensible boots?

**PEE-WEE:** In this town? Both.

**COWBOY CURTIS:** That's impossible.

**PEE-WEE:** Not with attitude it isn't.

---

**PEE-WEE:** I know what this day needs!

**EVERYONE:** What?!

**PEE-WEE:** The Secret Word!

**EVERYONE:** Aaaaaaahhhhhhh!

**PEE-WEE:** Today's secret word is...

*A drumroll appears from nowhere.*

**PEE-WEE:** **SNOW!**

Everybody loses their minds.

**CHAIRRY:** SNOW!

**CONKY:** SNOW! BEEP! SNOW! BEEP!

**MISS YVONNE:** Not on suede!

**COWBOY CURTIS:** Hide the grill!

**PTERRI:** I knew it! I knew it! I absolutely knew it!

---

*Pee-wee walks to the window and looks out at Milwaukee like he's personally trying to negotiate with it.*

**PEE-WEE:** So here's the deal, y'all. The daytime part of Saturday? Kind of a fake smile. The nighttime part? That's where the trouble starts. So enjoy the lull, keep your cocoa nearby, and maybe don't do that thing where one quiet afternoon tricks you into spiritual overconfidence.

**CONKY:** Final report! Milwaukee should remain flexible, layered, and just a little suspicious!

**PEE-WEE:** Ha-ha! That's good advice all the time!

**CHAIRRY:** Especially in Wisconsin!

**END.**

*The secret word was snow, and everybody was right to scream.*
