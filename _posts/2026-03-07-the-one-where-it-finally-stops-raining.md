*INT. BROCACH IRISH PUB - MORNING*

*RACHEL-ANN, MONIKA, PHOEBE-MARIE, JOEY-JOE, CHANDLER-CHUCK, and ROSS-ROBERT sit around their usual table near the window. Rain streaks down the glass. They all hold oversized mugs.*

**CHANDLER-CHUCK:** Could this rain BE any more annoying?

**MONIKA:** It's supposed to clear up this afternoon. I checked. **High of 55°F.** The sun's coming back.

**JOEY-JOE:** *(mouth full of cheese curds)* How do you just KNOW that?

**MONIKA:** I have a weather app. Like a normal person.

**JOEY-JOE:** I have a weather app too. It's called looking out the window.

*Laugh track.*

---

*INT. BROCACH - CONTINUOUS*

**ROSS-ROBERT:** Actually, what's happening is really interesting from a meteorological standpoint. Yesterday's warm front pushed through with that big rainstorm, and now we're getting the backside of the system. **Northwest winds** are going to clear things out. Classic post-frontal clearing pattern.

**RACHEL-ANN:** *(staring at her phone)* Uh huh.

**ROSS-ROBERT:** It's the same mechanism that causes lake-effect snow in November, just reversed with the seasonal—

**RACHEL-ANN:** Ross-Robert. Sweetie. Nobody asked.

*Laugh track.*

**PHOEBE-MARIE:** I actually felt the pressure change in my left knee this morning. **29.3 inches** and rising. I'm like a human barometer.

**CHANDLER-CHUCK:** That's... not a thing.

**PHOEBE-MARIE:** My knee says otherwise.

---

*EXT. THE MILWAUKEE RIVERWALK - AFTERNOON*

*The clouds break. Actual sunshine hits the river. MONIKA and RACHEL-ANN walk along the water.*

**RACHEL-ANN:** Oh my God, it's actually nice out.

**MONIKA:** **55°F.** I told you. Chance of rain this morning, then clearing. The afternoon is gorgeous.

**RACHEL-ANN:** This is the kind of day where you think spring is here and then Milwaukee hits you with a snowstorm next week.

**MONIKA:** Don't. Don't say it out loud. You'll jinx it.

---

*INT. JOEY-JOE AND CHANDLER-CHUCK'S APARTMENT - EVENING*

*A small apartment above a bar on KK Avenue. JOEY-JOE is trying to open his window.*

**JOEY-JOE:** I'm letting the fresh air in. It's March. It's practically summer.

**CHANDLER-CHUCK:** It's **dropping to 37°F** tonight.

**JOEY-JOE:** *(window now open, cold air blasting in)* ...I've made a huge mistake.

**CHANDLER-CHUCK:** Could you BE any worse at weather?

*Laugh track.*

---

*INT. BROCACH - NIGHT*

*The whole group is back. Through the window, stars are visible for the first time in days.*

**PHOEBE-MARIE:** *(picks up guitar)* Okay, I wrote a new song. It's called "Smelly Weather."

**EVERYONE:** No.

**PHOEBE-MARIE:** *Smelly weather, smell-y weather, what are they dumping in the Menomonee...*

**ROSS-ROBERT:** That's actually a valid environmental concern—

**RACHEL-ANN:** ROSS-ROBERT.

*Laugh track. Credits roll over a shot of the six of them walking along the lakefront, the skyline behind them, jackets zipped but smiling.*

**END.**

*We'll be there for you, Milwaukee. Even when the rain starts to pour.*
