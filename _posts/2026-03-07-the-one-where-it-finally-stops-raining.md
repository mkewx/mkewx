*INT. BROCACH IRISH PUB - MORNING*

*MEG, DANA, TESS, MARCO, CHARLIE, and GLENN sit around their usual table near the window. Rain streaks down the glass. They all hold oversized mugs.*

**CHARLIE:** Could this rain BE any more annoying?

**DANA:** It's supposed to clear up this afternoon. I checked. **High of 55°F.** The sun's coming back.

**MARCO:** *(mouth full of cheese curds)* How do you just KNOW that?

**DANA:** I have a weather app. Like a normal person.

**MARCO:** I have a weather app too. It's called looking out the window.

*Laugh track.*

---

*INT. BROCACH - CONTINUOUS*

**GLENN:** Actually, what's happening is really interesting from a meteorological standpoint. Yesterday's warm front pushed through with that big rainstorm, and now we're getting the backside of the system. **Northwest winds** are going to clear things out. Classic post-frontal clearing pattern.

**MEG:** *(staring at her phone)* Uh huh.

**GLENN:** It's the same mechanism that causes lake-effect snow in November, just reversed with the seasonal—

**MEG:** Glenn. Sweetie. Nobody asked.

*Laugh track.*

**TESS:** I actually felt the pressure change in my left knee this morning. **29.3 inches** and rising. I'm like a human barometer.

**CHARLIE:** That's... not a thing.

**TESS:** My knee says otherwise.

---

*EXT. THE MILWAUKEE RIVERWALK - AFTERNOON*

*The clouds break. Actual sunshine hits the river. DANA and MEG walk along the water.*

**MEG:** Oh my God, it's actually nice out.

**DANA:** **55°F.** I told you. Chance of rain this morning, then clearing. The afternoon is gorgeous.

**MEG:** This is the kind of day where you think spring is here and then Milwaukee hits you with a snowstorm next week.

**DANA:** Don't. Don't say it out loud. You'll jinx it.

---

*INT. MARCO AND CHARLIE'S APARTMENT - EVENING*

*A small apartment above a bar on KK Avenue. MARCO is trying to open his window.*

**MARCO:** I'm letting the fresh air in. It's March. It's practically summer.

**CHARLIE:** It's **dropping to 37°F** tonight.

**MARCO:** *(window now open, cold air blasting in)* ...I've made a huge mistake.

**CHARLIE:** Could you BE any worse at weather?

*Laugh track.*

---

*INT. BROCACH - NIGHT*

*The whole group is back. Through the window, stars are visible for the first time in days.*

**TESS:** *(picks up guitar)* Okay, I wrote a new song. It's called "Smelly Weather."

**EVERYONE:** No.

**TESS:** *Smelly weather, smell-y weather, what are they dumping in the Menomonee...*

**GLENN:** That's actually a valid environmental concern—

**MEG:** GLENN.

*Laugh track. Credits roll over a shot of the six of them walking along the lakefront, the skyline behind them, jackets zipped but smiling.*

**END.**

*We'll be there for you, Milwaukee. Even when the rain starts to pour.*
