Just a perfect day.
Walked out on the porch, forgot my jacket.
Didn't need it anyway.
**59 degrees** before the coffee kicked in,
and the sun just... sat there,
like it had nowhere else to be.

Just a perfect day.
Drove down to the lake with the windows cracked.
**Southwest wind, maybe 15.**
Enough to mess up your hair
but not enough to complain about.
The kind of breeze that makes you feel alive
even when you're just sitting in a parking lot
eating a gas station breakfast sandwich.

Oh, it's such a perfect day.
**63 by noon.**
They're saying we might touch **67**,
which would tie some record from 2016
that nobody remembers setting.
But we'll remember this one.

Just a perfect day.
Kids on bikes. Dogs off leash.
Somebody grilling on their balcony
like it's the Fourth of July
and not the ninth of March.
I saw a man in shorts and sandals
and honestly? I respected it.

You just keep me hanging on.
**Not a cloud worth mentioning.**
**Zero percent chance of rain.**
The kind of day where you call in sick
and don't even feel guilty about it.

Just a perfect day.
I'm glad I moved here.
Some days you wonder, y'all.
You really do.
The winters test you. The gray gets long.
But then Milwaukee gives you a Monday like this
and you think, okay.
Okay, I get it now.

*You're gonna reap just what you sow.*
*Tomorrow's not looking bad either.*
